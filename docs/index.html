THE ROUTE

This is real Cambridge, drawn pixel by pixel from OpenStreetMap. Every bus on it is a real bus, right now, moving along the real roads. All 700+ real bus stops are on the map too.

Spot buses on the map to fill your Busdex. Turn on go live (GPS) when you're out in Cambridge: stand near a real bus to catch it, or near a real stop to check in. That earns far more XP.

There are three daily missions, and they reset at midnight.
